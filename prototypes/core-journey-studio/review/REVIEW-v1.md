This is a rendered hierarchy problem, not just a minimum-size preference. **Impact:** fitting every block inside one desktop viewport currently takes priority over following the moving scene comfortably. A successful bounds assertion does not resolve that user-job regression. Phone width is appropriate to its device composition and closer viewing context; reproducing a smaller picture on a desktop is not equivalent acceptance.
